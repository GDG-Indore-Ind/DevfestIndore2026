# DevFest Indore attendee design system

Updated 20 November 2026. Direction: an orbital festival rooted in the Indore developer community. The organizer has now approved the supplied Figma references: bold DevFest typography, an इंदौर / 2026 pill, rounded artwork windows, and a single Android astronaut. This direction supersedes the earlier technical-editorial hero while retaining its clear copy, navigation and factual content.

## Foundations

Keep the established self-hosted Google Sans family (`--font-sans` lists a small `Google Sans Indic` face first: `src/app/fonts/google-sans-devanagari-subset.woff2`, declared in `globals.css` and limited by `unicode-range` to इंदौर and ₹, because the Latin file has neither; any new Devanagari text needs those glyphs added to the subset), 1160px container, 32px desktop / 20px mobile gutters, 4px spacing unit and `--audience-*` light/dark tokens. Display uses weights 500–600, with 700 reserved for the DevFest hero; body 400. Use the same Google Sans family for supporting labels and annotations; no monospace treatment. Use pill-shaped primary controls, 24px photograph/artwork windows and 12px topic controls. Prefer spacing and rules elsewhere. Keep the site uncluttered.

Light: #ffffff background, #202124 ink, #4c5159 secondary, #185abc action with white label. Dark: #10141d background, #f1f3f4 ink, #bdc5d3 secondary, #a8c7fa action with #102b54 label. Technical drawing uses #102434 with #dcebf5 ink and #b9d1e2 labels. Paper panels use #f5f7f8 light / #16202b dark. Root theme tokens live in `src/app/tokens.css`, imported by globals.css. Existing global layers load in order: globals, audience, engineering, space. New isolated component styles belong in colocated CSS Modules; shared utilities and tokens remain global. There is no admin/campaign UI.

Use 16px for body text and important form labels, at least 14px for practical metadata such as session times, 12px for supplementary captions, 18px for lead text and 32px for section headings. Compact page titles scale from 36px on phones to 48px on wide screens. Hero name is approximately 64–112px, set left above the compact Hindi city/year lockup. Small diagram labels are graphic annotation, not required event details. Date, venue and purchase action remain ordinary readable HTML.

## Layout rhythm and surfaces

Updated 3 November 2026. Pages share one rhythm from `src/app/tokens.css`: sections use `--section-space` (48px desktop, 32px mobile), dense follow-on blocks use `--section-space-tight` (32px / 24px) and every page intro uses `--intro-space` (32px / 24px). Section headings sit 16px above their content. Components take their padding from these tokens; do not add per-page section or intro overrides. Updated 4 November 2026: home sections are divided by one 1px `--audience-line` rule above each section after the hero (no per-section bottom borders), archive sections use the same rule, section headings sit 24–32px above their content, and list rows keep at least 16px of vertical padding.

Cards frame passes, people, partners and archive editions. Everything else is separated by spacing and 1px `--audience-line` rules. Compact cards use 20px padding (24px for pass cards), 16px between people cards and 24px between pass cards, with `--corner-16`; artwork and photograph windows keep 24px. Section headings use `--audience-h2` (the `--text-h3` scale), card titles 16–22px, metadata 13–14px muted. Every subpage uses the compact intro: page title at `--text-h1-sm`, a 16px lead and a 72px decorative mark from 761px up. Responsive layouts preserve the page’s main task and keep details paired with the content they describe.

## Visual language

One Android astronaut illustration carries the hero alongside a single four-point sparkle and a simple orbital motif in rounded windows. Keep generous white space in light mode and quiet dark surfaces in dark mode. Google blue, red, yellow and green are small signals; ticket purchase actions are yellow with dark ink and an outlined offset shadow. No star fields, neon glows or custom cursors. Artwork remains static; community and sponsor logos use stationary, scrollable rows. Artwork never implies an official product partnership or announced session. Keep original organization logos on white plates in both themes.

Remaining shared refinements live in `src/app/space.css`, imported after engineering.css. Hero artwork, appearance and referral styling live beside their components in CSS Modules. New artwork is `public/images/android-astronaut-2026.webp`, with transparent alpha and an approximately 184 KB payload. The source prompt and generation details are recorded in `docs/space-artwork-2026.md`.

## Material 3 influence

Use [Material 3 Expressive](https://design.google/library/expressive-material-design-google-research) to guide where the page puts emphasis. The hero and badge can use large typography and artwork. Schedules and directories use readable titles, grouped metadata and ruled rows. Save tinted surfaces for selected states and contextual callouts. Reserve filled yellow for ticket actions and use filled blue for the primary action in a tool, such as Download badge. Outlined pills support secondary actions.

## Key components

- **Space hero:** bold DevFest name, Hindi city/year pill, readable date with check-in time, venue, the lowest open pass price (“from” only when open prices differ; hidden when none is confirmed) and ordinary ticket action. One decorative Android astronaut in a rounded window, with two supporting geometric tiles. Static artwork; no motion control needed. Stacks on mobile.
- **Topic index:** four native pressed-state buttons form a row on desktop and a two-column grid on mobile. A tinted surface, border and check mark identify the selected theme; Left/Right/Home/End keys also select topics. Each choice reveals a story, orbital graphic and concrete topic tags. Motion is brief and interaction-driven, with a static reduced-motion equivalent. A short row follows with the agenda status, a See the agenda link and the speaker CTA. Label navigation Themes. Content changes in a polite live region.
- **ThemeChoice:** paired Sun/Moon native buttons, each 44px, with explicit accessible light/dark labels and pressed state. It sits in the header on wider screens and at the foot of the Explore menu on phones (760px and below); the choice persists locally. Existing default light and saved preferences remain.
- **Explore:** compact disclosure grouped into Plan your visit and Discover. Two columns on desktop; stacked groups with two-column links on mobile, scrollable within the viewport. Close on Escape/outside/navigation/focus leaving; Escape restores trigger focus.
- **History preview:** the Community section shows news clippings with links to the Archive’s press and photographs; there is no photo marquee. Ecosystem community logos appear after the venue.
- **Agenda:** `/agenda` is the single programme page. One pill row jumps to Sessions, Workshops, Activities and Exhibits. The SQLite programme is built by `src/content/agenda.ts` and ordered by time, then slot and speaker order. Each session, workshop or exhibit uses a ruled row showing the title, speaker and organization where known, followed by time, hall and duration when available. Expanding a row opens the description, format, track and speaker details. A single portrait grows from 44px to 96px (72px on phones), with reduced-motion support. Multiple presenters keep overlapping portraits. Speaker biographies are shown in full and profile links use LinkedIn, Instagram and X. Home’s “Meet the speakers” lineup still links directly to each person’s agenda entry. The Exhibits section contains the exhibit application link.
- **Badge maker:** `/badge` (`badge/BadgeMaker`, `badge/drawBadge`) exports one 1080 × 1080 PNG in the browser. The badge is a personal attendance announcement framed by a rounded 12px gradient border in the four Google colours. The large DevFest and official GDG Indore lockups share a header row above a two-column composition. The attendee’s photo or initials fill the large blue-outlined astronaut visor on the left. “I’m attending” introduces the name, designation and affiliation on the right. Names are limited to 64 characters and wrap within the column. A small Rajwada mark sits beside the date and city in the footer. The Mission panel starts just below the helmet. The chosen line uses the same headline style as the name and wraps across the badge, with an inset rule above the date and site address. The preview and PNG follow the site’s light or dark setting. The logo artwork stays intact and its wordmarks switch to light lettering on the dark badge. The preview sits beside the fields on wide screens and below them on phones. City sits beside the prefilled name. Mission has a full-width searchable picker with ten choices and a shaded 3D die button for another choice. Download PNG is the filled blue action. Copy caption is a separate action, and View caption opens the selectable text. Share image opens the system sheet when it supports files. The preview is an image so it can be saved with a long press. A link to the Mission Crew challenge follows the maker, and the homepage links to the badge in the hero and after passes.
- **Home on phones:** below 600px the speaker cards and below 560px the four “In the news” clippings each form one swipeable, snap-aligned row with the next card peeking, and below 761px the exhibit invitation drops its decorative illustration. Together these take the 375px home page from about 10,400px to 8,200px. Desktop layouts are unchanged.
- **Section header:** `shared/SectionHeader` heads every home section: title (32px) and a one-line lead on the left, at most one outlined “see all” action on the right (below the lead on phones). Blocks inside a section, such as press, photo archive and communities in the Community section, use its level-3 form (22px). Mission and Exhibits share one bordered panel surface; Passes stays the single dark band.
- **Breadcrumbs:** `shared/Breadcrumbs` appears only on nested pages (Home › Agenda › Workshops, Home › Past editions › DevFest Indore 2025), above the meta line, 14px muted with the current page in ink. It also emits `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD. Top-level pages show none.
- **Chips:** `shared/Chip` is a 24px tonal pill: text in the theme’s `--icon-*` colour on a 12% tint of the same colour. The label always names the format; colour only reinforces it.
- **Archive:** `/archive` shows DevFest and Cloud Community Days editions together, newest first. Edition cards use one column on phones. The archive also includes searchable campus workshops, community events, photos and a press preview that links to `/press`. An edition page uses breadcrumbs and one row of section links, with its cover beside the summary when there is a cover. Edition pages contain speakers and talks, photographs, pertinent press and partner communities. Undated historical sponsors are omitted.
- **People:** Team, Ambassadors and campus workshop speakers use `shared/PersonRoster`. Ruled rows use a 44px round photo or initials, a 16px name, 14px metadata and profile icons. The roster uses two columns on wider screens and one on phones. A chevron beside the person's name opens their biography and participation details when available. Team separates organizers and volunteers and leads with each person's first responsibility.
- **Ambassadors:** the searchable directory comes first. A link beside the result count jumps to the three code steps, with Get tickets next to the booking step. Ambassadors and Missions share the ruled `NumberedSteps` component. Each row shows LinkedIn and, where available, Instagram.
- **Compact people:** 72×80 portraits, two-column desktop / one-column mobile rows, all matching speakers in one scrolling list. Group multiple appearances by normalized person name. Biographies and session abstracts stay behind disclosures.
- **Organizations:** five-column desktop / two-column mobile sponsor logo wall, with all sponsors visible. Event partners follow as a separate compact name wall; source records remain in canonical content; the public archive does not show an artwork/source disclosure. Group repeated names across years within the same relationship.
- **Workshops:** `/agenda#workshops` lists event-day workshops and the first-come seating policy. Both former workshop routes redirect there. Past campus workshops live at `/archive#workshops`, with search and photographs. `/events` redirects to the archive section. Keep dates absent when not supplied.
- **Venue:** check-in time sits beside directions in the intro. The map and entry checklist follow.
- **Partners & collaborators:** the Partners intro links separately to the current lineup when published and the partner inquiry. Confirmed partners use one large card per slide on Home and Partners. A single revealed partner stays static. Exactly two partners sit side by side from 1000px, with the logo above the copy and no position dots. Otherwise multiple partners support touch swiping, keyboard arrows and larger position dots below the cards. Partner order follows `order` in `organizations.json` (currently Indore Institute of Science and Technology as platinum partner, Space Club of India, then Qualcomm). Space Club uses a left-side logo and a subtle star texture; technology partners can use a faint chip watermark behind card content. Logos remain on white plates. Individual partnership `visible` flags hold announcements back independently of the overall reveal switch.
- **Icons and outbound links:** Lucide 18px / stroke 1.75 for interface actions, hidden from assistive technology beside descriptive labels. Social links use platform marks with accessible names. Outbound web links use ExternalLink and announce a new tab.

## Accessibility and performance

Use one h1 per page; proper headings, real links, native disclosures and labeled filters. Maintain 44px action targets, visible focus, keyboard access, reduced-motion behavior and no horizontal overflow at 375px. Portraits request appropriately small image sizes. Real photographs below the fold load lazily.

Existing token contrast: light ink 16.10:1, secondary 7.99:1, primary action 6.51:1; dark ink 16.55:1, secondary 10.61:1, primary action 8.17:1. New drawing and paper combinations are checked with relative luminance in validation. Verified new combinations: drawing text 13.03:1; drawing labels 10.03:1; light paper secondary 7.43:1; dark paper secondary 9.48:1. Fine diagram construction lines need not carry the event’s factual content.

## Content and release rules

Canonical event facts live in `content/devfest.sqlite`; passes reference their inclusion sets and event policy wording belongs to `events.registration_policies`. Render passes using their explicit visibility flags, with no local release schedule. Show confirmed prices and sold-out labels, concise inclusions grouped by event access, food and goodies, and lounge access, with visible eligibility and disclosed conditions; keep Last Orbit and Observer hidden until approved. Do not hardcode a currently active pass in components or documentation.

Sessionize owns proposals; KonfHub owns checkout. Preserve eligibility, final-sale wording, referral behavior and the booking switch. Only publish confirmed participation; show historical contributors with edition context. `pages.partners.revealed` controls the public partner announcement.

## Main-content actions and color

Prominent destinations within main content use `shared/ContentAction`: outlined pill links with descriptive labels, a colored Lucide icon and a directional arrow. Filled yellow controls are reserved for the ticket path. Inline references, directory rows and header/footer links keep their existing treatment. `ContentIcon` supplies 20px action icons using `--icon-blue`, `--icon-green`, `--icon-red` and `--icon-yellow`; each has a light/dark token. Section headings have no icon accents. Themes is the navigation label for the topic explorer; agenda and speaker status remains explicit. Home rail stops follow page order: Launch, Themes, Speakers, Passes, Partners, Exhibits, Venue and Community, with Passes present only while the ticket strip renders. The mobile rocket rail shows the current section name above its path, with every destination still named for keyboard and assistive technology.

Use `shared/ContentActionGroup` for rows of related page-end actions, and `shared/PageSectionNavigation` for the pill navigation within archive and workshop pages. `shared/SocialGlyph` supplies the Instagram and LinkedIn marks used in both the footer and personal profiles.

Icon accent contrast against the page background (light / dark): blue 6.51 / 10.72, green 6.51 / 10.77, red 6.54 / 7.71, yellow 5.59 / 13.10. Icons remain decorative beside descriptive labels.

## Exhibit invitation

Home places a single mixed exhibit invitation after Partners and before Venue, with its own Exhibits rocket stop and Explore link. Software demos, physical AI, space ideas and other projects share one invitation for developers, makers, startups and companies. The panel uses a static illustration and one “Exhibit at DevFest” interest-form action; it lists only confirmed exhibits by name (currently Qualcomm × Arduino demos and Space Club of India), with no demo counts, guaranteed space, pricing or benefits. The current event's `links.exhibitApplication` owns the form URL; `pages.home.exhibits` owns the invitation copy. Themes styling now lives entirely in `home/ExploreSection.module.css`.

Help and policy links use shared `SupportLinks` with 24px horizontal and 8px vertical gaps, 44px link targets and natural wrapping. Before you book uses plain policy headings and paragraphs, including the LinkedIn reminder; do not turn it into a highlighted panel.

Explore uses a decorative 18px Lucide icon beside each text label, with one accessible accent color per group. Plan your visit includes the agenda, FAQ and ambassador contact; Discover covers the current event and community and ends with one Past editions link to the archive. Keep each group concise and retain the text labels and 44px link targets.

The footer uses three aligned navigation groups with small decorative icons, a labelled help email, and a separate community row with social icons and the chapter link. The code-of-conduct link sits beside the closing disclaimer. Social icons retain accessible platform names and 44px targets; keep all destinations visible on mobile.

Progress rails appear only on pages with at least four section stops. The home passes strip uses a deep blue background in both themes. Token namespace consolidation for `--audience-*`, `--space-*`, `--mission-*` and `--engineering-*` remains a follow-up.
