About DevFest
इंदौर

DevFest is a global, community-driven tech conference hosted by Google

Developer Groups (GDG) around the world — a celebration of innovation,
learning, and collaboration. Every DevFest is uniquely crafted by local GDG
organizers to reflect the learning needs, ambitions, and creative spirit of their
regional developer ecosystem

DevFest 2026 Indore, an initiative by GDG Indore, stands as Central India’s
largest developer conference, uniting developers, students, startups, and
technology enthusiasts under one roof.
This year’s edition focuses on AI, Cloud, and emerging technologies, driving
impactful knowledge exchange and meaningful networking opportunities.

Through a blend of keynotes, hands-on sessions, and expert panels, DevFest
Indore aims to empower the next generation of innovators while providing brands
and partners an unparalleled opportunity to connect with the brightest tech
minds shaping the future.

About Us
GDG Indore, which stands for Google Developers Group Indore, is a
technical-community based in Indore, India. We organize frequent
meetup events which include talks on a wide range of technical topics
where you can learn new skills through hands-on workshops, online
challenges, interaction with the community, and hackathons.
The community prides itself on being an inclusive environment where
everyone and anyone interested in tech - from beginner developers to
experienced professionals - all are welcome to join.
To learn more about the GDG program, visit
https://developers.google.com/community/gdg

Hands-on Workshop Series
India

A collaborative initiative by GDG Indore, this ongoing Hands-On Workshop Series
is designed to bring real-world tech learning directly to campuses and corporate
spaces.
Partnering with Google Developer Groups on Campus (GDGoCs) across leading
institutes and industry offices, we aim to bridge the gap between theory and
practice through interactive, project-based learning sessions on cutting-edge
technologies — from AI and Cloud Computing to Web, Mobile, and GenAI.
Each workshop provides participants with:
⚙ Practical, hands-on experience guided by industry experts
🌐 Exposure to real-world tools and technologies
🤝 Collaboration opportunities between students and professionals
🚀 Pathways to contribute to the larger developer ecosystem
Together, we’re building a community where learning meets doing —

one campus at a time
