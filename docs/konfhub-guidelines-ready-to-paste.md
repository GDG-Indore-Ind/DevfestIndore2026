# DevFest Indore 2026 — Registration guidelines

Saturday, 28 November 2026 · Essentia Luxury Hotel Indore

Registration opens at 8:30 AM IST.

## Before you book

**Add your LinkedIn URL before booking.** For smoother registration, sign in to KonfHub and add your LinkedIn profile URL, full name and mobile number to your profile before booking. Use your own profile URL when entering attendee details.

**One pass per person.** Use a unique name, email address and LinkedIn profile for each attendee so their digital credential and QR code are issued correctly.

**Tell us your food preference.** Choose Veg or Jain Veg on the registration form to help us plan catering at Essentia.

Food preferences apply only to passes that include catering.

## Choose the right pass

### Liftoff Pass

The first release, open to all developers and community members.

Open to all developers and community members. Maximum 2 tickets per order. Each ticket must have a different attendee’s details.

**Included:** Full-day admission across all technical tracks; Keynote sessions; Official DevFest Indore swag kit; Lunch and refreshments.

### Cadet Pass

For currently enrolled undergraduate and postgraduate students.

For currently enrolled college and university students only. Enter your college name, degree and graduation year at checkout. Bring your original, active physical student photo ID, matching your registration name. Maximum 1 ticket per order.

**Included:** Full-day admission across all technical tracks; Keynote sessions; Student delegate kit; Lunch and refreshments.

Working professionals booking this pass may have their registration cancelled or be required to pay the difference for a Commander upgrade at the venue. Missing valid student ID may mean denied entry without refund or an on-site upgrade fee.

### Commander Pass

For engineers, architects, tech leads, managers and founders.

For working software engineers, architects, tech leads, engineering managers and founders. Register each team member with their own name, email address and LinkedIn profile.

**Included:** Full-day conference admission and breakout room access; Priority partner networking zones; Delegate kit, lunch and refreshments; Automatic team discounts at checkout; Corporate tax invoice available at checkout.

Volume discounts apply when booking multiple passes for engineering teams and startups. The checkout will show the applicable discount.

### Flight Deck Pass

Curated access for founders, senior technical leaders and ecosystem partners.

Curated for founders, senior technical leadership and ecosystem partners. A private VIP access code is required to unlock booking in the ticket section on KonfHub.

**Included:** Dedicated, expedited VIP registration desk; Reserved front-row seating across keynotes and tracks; All-day Speaker & Partner Networking Lounge access; Exclusive evening mixer; Premium VIP delegate kit.

Have a private VIP access code? Enter it in the KonfHub ticket section when this release opens. The code does not replace a completed registration.

### Last Orbit Pass

A final chance to join, subject to venue capacity.

General registration for late-decision attendees. Allocation is subject to final venue hall capacity.

**Included:** Full-day technical track access; Conference lunch.

Final-phase surge pricing applies. Delegate kits and swag are first come, first served, while supplies last.

### Observer Pass

For community walk-ins attending main auditorium keynotes and open tracks.

For community walk-ins and session-only attendees.

**Included:** Main auditorium keynote admission; Open technical tracks.

No conference lunch, tea or refreshments, workshop hall access, or delegate swag kit.

## Registration and attendance policies

### 1. All purchases are final

Tickets cannot be cancelled, transferred, resold, rescheduled or refunded, including for personal scheduling conflicts or corporate travel changes.

### 2. Entry and identification

Bring your digital QR ticket and an original physical government-issued photo ID matching the attendee name on your registration. Present them at check-in for verification and badge collection.

### 3. Accurate attendee details

Provide each attendee’s own full name, email address, mobile number and LinkedIn profile URL. Your student or professional status and other eligibility information must be true and accurate. Organizers may verify details and supporting identification. False, misleading or unverifiable information may result in cancellation or refusal of entry.

### 4. Cadet student ID check

Cadet is for currently enrolled college and university students. Bring your original, active physical institutional photo ID matching your registration name, in addition to the general entry requirements. Without valid proof, organizers may deny entry without refund or require an on-site upgrade fee. Working professionals booking Cadet may have their registration cancelled or need to pay the difference for a Commander upgrade.

### 5. Workshop access and capacity

Check whether your selected pass includes workshop hall access. Where included, workshop seats are subject to capacity and any announced session-specific access or registration requirements. A conference ticket alone does not reserve a workshop seat.

### 6. Kits, catering and pass inclusions

Admission, food, refreshments, workshops and delegate kits are provided according to the inclusions of your selected pass. Any stated first-come, first-served or while-supplies-last conditions apply to the specified pass only.

### 7. Flight Deck private access

Flight Deck is a curated pass requiring a private VIP access code in the KonfHub ticket section. A code or expression of interest does not replace a completed registration. Only the benefits listed for this pass are included.

### 8. Registration corrections

If your own registration details contain an error, contact the organizers before the event. A correction request does not permit transferring a ticket to another person or changing the attendee’s identity.

### 9. A welcoming community

DevFest Indore is an inclusive, harassment-free conference. All attendees, speakers, partners and volunteers must follow the community code of conduct and organizer instructions. Organizers may revoke passes or require people to leave for harassment, disruptive behaviour or other conduct violations, without a refund.
