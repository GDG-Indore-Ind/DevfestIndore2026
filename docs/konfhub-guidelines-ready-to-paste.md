## Before you book

**Add your LinkedIn URL.** Sign in to KonfHub and add your own LinkedIn profile URL before booking.

## Choose the right pass

### LiftOff Pass

Full-day access for developers and community members.

Open to everyone. Up to 2 tickets per order, with separate details for each attendee.

**Included:** Sessions; Workshop access (First come, first served); Lunch & refreshments; DevFest Goodies; Community Lounge; Expert Lounge; Exhibit Lounge.

### Cadet Pass

Full-day access for college and university students.

Currently enrolled students only. Add your college, degree and graduation year at checkout. Bring your original, active physical student photo ID matching your registration. Limit: 1 ticket per order.

**Included:** Sessions; Workshop access (First come, first served); Lunch & refreshments; DevFest Goodies; Community Lounge; Expert Lounge; Exhibit Lounge.

Without valid student ID, entry may be denied without refund or an upgrade fee may apply. Professionals booking Cadet may face cancellation or need to pay for a Commander upgrade.

### Commander Pass

Full-day access for working professionals.

Working professionals only, including engineers, architects, tech leads, managers and founders. Students must choose Cadet. Register every attendee with their own details.

**Included:** Sessions; Workshop access (First come, first served); Lunch & refreshments; DevFest Goodies; Community Lounge; Expert Lounge; Exhibit Lounge.

Team discounts and a corporate tax invoice are available at checkout. Check KonfHub for the applicable discount.

### Flight Deck Pass

Additional access for invited founders, technical leaders and ecosystem partners.

After booking Cadet or Commander, express interest in Flight Deck. Invitations are limited; booking a standard pass does not guarantee VIP access.

**Included:** Priority access; Sessions; Workshop access (First come, first served); Lunch with delegates (Refreshments included); DevFest Goodies; Community Lounge; Expert Lounge; Exhibit Lounge.

Follow the registration instructions in your invitation.

### Last Orbit Pass

Late registration, subject to venue capacity.

Open to everyone, subject to final venue capacity.

**Included:** Sessions; Lunch.

Late-booking pricing applies. DevFest Goodies are first come, first served, while supplies last.

### Observer Pass

Keynotes and open technical sessions only.

Open to community walk-ins and session-only attendees.

**Included:** Main auditorium keynotes; Open technical sessions.

Lunch, tea, refreshments, workshop hall access and DevFest Goodies are not included.

## Registration and attendance policies

### 1. All sales are final

Tickets cannot be cancelled, transferred, resold, rescheduled or refunded.

### 2. Bring your ticket and photo ID

Bring your digital QR ticket and an original physical government-issued photo ID matching your registration name for check-in and badge collection.

### 3. Use each attendee’s own details

Enter each attendee’s full name, email, mobile number and LinkedIn URL. Give accurate student or professional details. Organizers may check your information and ID; false or unverifiable details may lead to cancellation or denied entry.

### 4. Cadet: student ID required

Cadet is for currently enrolled college and university students. Bring your original, active physical student photo ID matching your registration, alongside your government photo ID. Without valid proof, entry may be denied without refund or an upgrade fee may apply. Professionals booking Cadet may face cancellation or need to pay for a Commander upgrade.

### 5. Workshop seats are limited

Your pass must include workshop access. Seats depend on capacity and any session-specific registration requirements. A conference ticket does not reserve a workshop seat.

### 6. What your pass includes

Access, meals, refreshments and DevFest Goodies depend on your selected pass. Any first-come, first-served or while-supplies-last condition applies only where stated for that pass.

### 7. Flight Deck: invitation only

Book Cadet or Commander before expressing interest in Flight Deck. Invitations are limited and not guaranteed. Follow the registration instructions in your invitation; expressing interest is not a confirmed registration.

### 8. Need to correct your details?

Contact the organizers before the event. Corrections cannot transfer a ticket or change the attendee’s identity.

### 9. Respect the community

Everyone must follow the code of conduct and organizer instructions. Harassment, disruptive behaviour or other violations may result in a revoked pass or removal without refund.
