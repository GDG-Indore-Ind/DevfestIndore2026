# GDG DevFest Indore 2026 🚀

**DevFest** is the flagship annual developer conference hosted by **Google Developer Groups (GDG)** worldwide. It brings together practitioners, researchers, software engineers, and founders for a full day of multi-track deep dives into **Applied AI/ML, Cloud Architecture, Modern Systems Design, and Physical AI**.

Join us at **Essentia Luxury Hotel, Indore** on **November 28, 2026**, for keynote addresses, technical tracks, hands-on labs, and high-impact community networking.

## ⚡ Registration & Checkout Checklist

1. **Pre-Drop Profile Prep:** To secure your ticket during high-demand drops (such as the *Liftoff Pass*), ensure you log into your [KonfHub](https://konfhub.com) account and pre-fill your **Full Name, Mobile Number, and LinkedIn Profile URL**. Having an active profile ensures one-click auto-fill during checkout.
2. **One Pass Per Person:** Passes are personalized. Provide unique names, email addresses, and LinkedIn profiles for each attendee so individual digital credentials and QR codes generate accurately.
3. **Food Preference:** Please specify your dietary requirements (Veg / Jain Veg) on the registration form to assist our catering team at Essentia.
