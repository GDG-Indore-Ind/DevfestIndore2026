All seven website assets total 745,992 bytes. Originals were orientation-corrected, resized inside 1400 × 1000 pixels without upscaling and encoded as WebP quality 78. Source images and photographer marks were not retouched. Raw camera files stay outside the repository. Public records retain accurate dimensions, captions and source links for new additions. The site serves checked-in images and does not depend on Drive downloads at runtime.

The 2014 history origin comes from the organizer's statement in this chat; none of these samples is presented as a 2014 photograph. The timeline links only existing 2022–2025 edition records. Cloud Community Days photos remain explicitly identified separately from DevFest.
